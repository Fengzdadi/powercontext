Pour-over coffee is brewed by pouring hot water slowly over ground coffee in a paper or metal filter. A common starting recipe is a 1:15 ratio, for example 20 grams of medium-fine coffee with 300 grams of water at about 92 to 96 degrees Celsius.

Start with a bloom: pour roughly twice the coffee's weight in water and wait 30 to 45 seconds so trapped carbon dioxide can escape. Then pour in slow circles until the target weight is reached. A total brew time of about three minutes usually gives a balanced cup; grind finer if it tastes sour and coarser if it tastes bitter.
